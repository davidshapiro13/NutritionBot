What you’ll need (serves 4)
- 1 cup dried brown or green lentils (not pre‑cooked)  
- 1 cup brown rice (or 2 cups cooked from a bag)  
- 1 medium onion, chopped  
- 2 carrots, diced  
- 2 stalks celery, diced (optional)  
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes (or 2 fresh tomatoes, chopped)  
- 3 cups low‑sodium vegetable or chicken broth (or water + bouillon)  
- 2 cups fresh spinach or chopped kale (can use frozen)  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 tsp ground cumin (or smoked paprika) + salt & pepper to taste  
- Optional: a squeeze of lemon, hot sauce, or a spoonful of plain yogurt for serving

Approximate cost in Somerville: $6–8 total if you buy basic brands (dried lentils and rice, canned tomatoes, simple produce) — about $1.50–$2 per serving. Dried lentils and rice store well and are the cheapest per serving staples.

How to make it (about 35–40 minutes)
1. Rinse lentils and brown rice.  
2. In a large pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté onion, carrot, and celery 5–7 minutes until softened. Add garlic and spices, cook 30 seconds.  
3. Add lentils, canned tomatoes (with juices), and broth. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer. Cover and cook 20–25 minutes until lentils are tender. If you prefer, cook rice separately according to package directions (about 40 min) or use quick‑cook brown rice (20 min).  
4. Stir in spinach/kale at the end and cook 2–3 minutes until wilted. Taste and season with salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon. Serve over brown rice.

- Balanced meal: Complex carbs (brown rice), protein (lentils), healthy fat (olive oil), and plenty of vegetables—good for sustained energy and satiety.

Money‑saving & time tips

* 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 onion, diced

* 2 cloves garlic, minced

* 1 teaspoon chili powder
* 1/2 teaspoon paprika
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* 4 whole wheat tortillas
* 1 cup enchilada sauce (homemade or store-bought)
* Shredded cheese (optional)

Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic. Cook until the vegetables are tender, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the diced sweet potato and cook for an additional 5 minutes, or until it starts to soften.
4. Stir in the black beans, cumin, chili powder, and paprika. Cook for 1-2 minutes, until the flavors are combined.
5. In a separate pan, warm the tortillas over medium heat for about 30 seconds on each side. This will make them more pliable and easier to roll.
6. To assemble the enchiladas, lay a tortilla flat and spoon some of the sweet potato and black bean mixture onto the center of the tortilla. Roll the tortilla and place it seam-side down in a baking dish. Repeat with the remaining tortillas and filling.
7. Pour the enchilada sauce over the rolled tortillas and top with shredded cheese (if using).
8. Bake the enchiladas in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
