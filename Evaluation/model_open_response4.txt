* 1 bag of mixed frozen vegetables (such as broccoli, carrots, and bell peppers) ($1.50)
* 1 bag of baby spinach leaves ($1.50)
* 1 bottle of vinaigrette dressing ($2.00)
* Salt, pepper, and olive oil for seasoning

**Total Cost:** $10.00

This leaves you with $10.00 for any additional ingredients or snacks you might want to add to the meal.

**Tips for Stretching Your Budget:**

* Consider buying chicken breast in bulk and freezing it for future meals.

* Opt for a simple vinaigrette dressing instead of a more expensive salad dressing.
* Use whole wheat pasta to get more fiber and nutrients.

**Cooking Instructions:**

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Cook the whole wheat pasta according to the package instructions.
3. Toss the frozen vegetables with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and spread them out on a baking sheet. Roast in the oven for 15-20 minutes, or until tender.
4. Grill the chicken breast until cooked through, then slice it into strips.
5. In a large bowl, combine the cooked pasta, roasted vegetables, and sliced chicken breast.
6. Top with baby spinach leaves and a drizzle of vinaigrette dressing.
