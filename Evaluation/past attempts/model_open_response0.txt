One of my favorite cheap and healthy meal ideas is a simple yet satisfying Black Bean and Sweet Potato Tacos. Here's a recipe you can try:

Ingredients:

* 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and diced
* 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 small onion, diced
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* 1 teaspoon cumin
* 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* 4 corn tortillas
* Optional toppings: diced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, diced avocado, salsa, shredded cheese

Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Toss the sweet potato with 1/2 tablespoon olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for about 20-25 minutes, or until tender.
3. In a large skillet, heat the remaining 1/2 tablespoon olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until softened, about 3-4 minutes. Add the garlic, cumin, and chili powder, and cook for another minute.
4. Add the black beans to the skillet and stir to combine with the onion mixture. Cook for about 2-3 minutes, or until heated through.
5. Warm the tortillas by wrapping them in a damp paper towel and microwaving for 20-30 seconds.
6. Assemble the tacos by spooning the black bean mixture onto a tortilla, followed by roasted sweet potato, and any desired toppings.

Why is this meal healthy?

* Sweet potatoes are rich in vitamin A, fiber, and antioxidants, making them an excellent source of nutrition.
* Black beans are high in protein, fiber, and folate, making them a great plant-based protein source.
* The cumin and chili powder add a boost of antioxidants and anti-inflammatory compounds.
* The whole grain corn tortillas provide fiber and B vitamins.
* The toppings offer a range of vitamins and minerals, including vitamin C from the tomatoes and vitamin E from the avocado.
