As a nutritionist in Boston, I'm happy to share a delicious, affordable, and nutritious dinner idea that's perfect for a Somerville resident like you.

One of my favorite cheap and healthy meal options is a classic Black Bean and Sweet Potato Tacos recipe. Here's why:

**Cost:** This meal is budget-friendly, with most ingredients costing under $5. You can find sweet potatoes and black beans at a reasonable price at local grocery stores like Market Basket or Aldi.

**Health Benefits:**

1. **Sweet Potatoes:** Rich in vitamin A (beta-carotene), vitamin C, and fiber, sweet potatoes are an excellent source of antioxidants and complex carbohydrates. They're also low on the glycemic index, making them a great choice for blood sugar control.
2. **Black Beans:** These legumes are packed with protein, fiber, and folate, making them an excellent plant-based protein source. Black beans are also rich in minerals like potassium, magnesium, and iron.
3. **Tacos:** Using whole wheat or whole grain tacos shells (or even making your own with whole wheat tortillas) provides additional fiber and B vitamins.

**Recipe:**

Ingredients:

* 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
* 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 onion, diced
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 teaspoon cumin
* 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
* Salt and pepper to taste
* 4-6 whole wheat tacos shells
* Optional toppings: diced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, diced avocado, salsa, shredded cheese

Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Toss the sweet potatoes with olive oil, cumin, and chili powder on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes or until tender.
3. In a pan, sauté the onion and black beans with a pinch of salt and pepper until the onion is translucent.
4. Warm the tacos shells according to package instructions.
5. Assemble the tacos by spooning the black bean mixture onto a shell, followed by roasted sweet potatoes, and your desired toppings.

**Nutritional Breakdown (approximate):**

Per serving (2 tacos):

* Calories: 400
* Protein: 15g
* Fat: 10g
* Saturated Fat: 1.5g
* Carbohydrates: 60g
* Fiber: 10g
* Sugar: 10g
* Sodium: 200mg

This recipe is not only delicious, but it's also packed with nutrients, fiber, and protein, making it an excellent choice for a healthy and affordable dinner in Somerville. Enjoy!
