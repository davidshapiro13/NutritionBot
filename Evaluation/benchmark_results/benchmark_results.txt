RAG1: How long can eggnog last open in the fridge?
Answer: Short answer: it depends.

- Store-bought pasteurized eggnog (opened and kept at ≤40°F): 5–7 days.
- Shelf-stable/UHT eggnog (once opened): treat like above — about 5–7 days.
- Homemade eggnog made with raw eggs: be conservative — 24–48 hours in the fridge (raw eggs raise Salmonella risk).
- Homemade eggnog that’s been cooked/tempered to 160°F: about 3–4 days refrigerated.

Tips: keep it tightly covered, store at ≤40°F, and don’t leave it out over 2 hours. Alcohol can slow spoilage a little but won’t make it safe indefinitely. Throw it out if it smells sour, is curdled, discolored, or has mold. You can also freeze eggnog (texture may change) for up to ~3–6 months.

Why it’s good
- Lean, high‑quality protein (3–4 oz cooked ≈ 20–30 g protein) for muscle, satiety, weight control.
- Rich in B vitamins, selenium and zinc.
- Lower in fat than many red meats if you choose white meat and remove the skin.

Things to watch
- Processed turkey (deli meat, pre‑seasoned or brined birds) can be high in sodium and additives — pick low‑sodium or plain and check labels.
- Dark meat has more fat and calories than breast; cooking method matters (roast, grill, bake are better than frying).
- If you have kidney disease, gout, or a sodium‑sensitive heart condition, talk to your clinician about portion and frequency.

Quick healthy dinner ideas
- Roast turkey breast with roasted vegetables and a whole grain.
- Turkey burger (no frying) with salad and sweet potato.
- Stir‑fry with lots of veggies and brown rice.

Leftovers (practical)
- Refrigerate within 2 hours; use within 3–4 days, or freeze 2–3 months.
