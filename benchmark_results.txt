RAG1: How long can eggnog last open in the fridge?
Answer: Short answer: It depends.

- Store-bought, pasteurized eggnog (opened): 5–7 days in the fridge at or below 40°F (4°C).  
- Homemade with raw eggs: 24–48 hours.  
- Homemade that’s been cooked/tempered to 160°F (pasteurized by heat): about 3–4 days.  
- If it’s spiked with a lot of liquor it may keep longer, but alcohol under ~20% won’t reliably preserve it.

Always keep the container tightly closed, refrigerate promptly, and discard if it smells sour, looks curdled/separated, or shows mold. If it sat out over 2 hours (1 hour in hot weather), toss it. Want tips on storing or using up leftover eggnog?
