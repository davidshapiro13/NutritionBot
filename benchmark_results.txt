RAG1: How long can eggnog last open in the fridge?
Answer: If it’s store‑bought, pasteurized eggnog: once opened keep it refrigerated at ≤40°F (4°C) and use within about 5–7 days (or by the date on the carton, whichever comes first).

If it’s homemade:
- Made with raw (unpasteurized) eggs: treat as high‑risk — use within 24–48 hours and avoid if you’re pregnant or immunocompromised.
- Cooked (tempered/cooked) homemade eggnog: about 3–4 days in the fridge.

Always discard if it smells sour, looks curdled or moldy, tastes off, or was left out at room temperature more than 2 hours. If you’re unsure, throw it out.
